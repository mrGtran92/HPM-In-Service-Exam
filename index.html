A laptop or desktop is strongly recommended. This exam has long clinical scenarios with five options each and takes 90–120 minutes. You may continue on this device, but it will be harder to read.

Before you begin

  • Single attempt. Once submitted, answers cannot be changed.
  • Move freely between questions and change answers any time before submitting.
  • No feedback during the exam — answers and rationales appear after you submit.
  • Reference (top right) opens lab reference ranges, the equianalgesic table and a calculator in a window you can move and resize.
  • Cross out choices you have ruled out, and select words in a question to highlight them.
  • Your answers save automatically. If your browser closes or your computer restarts, reopen this page and you can pick up where you left off.

Keyboard: A–E choose · ←→ move · F flag · R all questions · Shift+A–E cross out · L reference
